# No deviations: the pre-registration is not in force yet.

A deviation is any departure from the plan in force on data already collected. Each one is recorded here, following the template of Willroth and Atherton (2024):

- **type**;
- **reason**;
- **timing**, with block and date;
- **original plan**;
- **deviation**;
- **impact** on the interpretation;
- for errors in the analysis code, **whether they emerged before or after E3 was computed** (Annex D).

Changes due to data protection and undeclared removals (section 13) also go here. The impact classes are in the [pre-registration](preregistration.md), section 14.
